IEEE at
UBC Okanagan.

The UBCO IEEE Student Branch aims to help STEM students transition from their educational to professional pursuits. We support aspiring engineers by providing opportunities and experiences to learn beyond the classroom. Through workshops, networking events, and dedicated lab space, we hope to foster a close-knit and encouraging environment for students to discover their passion and interest in engineering.
